# Holiday Drift

*A psychogeographic dérive through twenty-four hours of a rented house near Angoulême — compiled from field notes, diary fragments, chalkboard residue, and the four song-vessels of the day. Method: the Situationist dérive. Object: not the holiday as spectacle, but the ambiences that seize the body when it refuses the itinerary.*

> The dérive is a technique of rapid passage through varied ambiences. It is entirely different from the classic notions of journey or stroll. In a dérive one or more persons during a certain period drop their relations, their work and leisure activities, and all their other usual motives for movement and action, and let themselves be drawn by the attractions of the terrain and the encounters they find there.
>
> — Guy Debord, *Theory of the Dérive* (1956)

What follows is not a diary. It is a map of atmospheres arranged by the clock — a unitary calendar of one domestic microclimate: stone walls four centuries deep, a pool that is neither land nor sea, shutters that open and close like eyelids, chalk that flows like a river toward the sea.

---

## 00:00 — Silence after the last voice

The house falls silent. History continues embedding itself in the walls. Latin and Germanic words mingle in the mortar. The air conditioner — that small machine of recuperated leisure — still sucks unwanted heat from the bedrooms so that sleep, always provisional, can approach.

Outside: insect bites. Warm skin. Alcoholic blood. The night feeds.

Unresolved: sleeping in the heat. How does one relax when relaxation itself has become a product?

---

## 01:00 — Neurological geography of the dark

Travelling into the past down the spinal cord. Memories genetically encoded — neurotic past as terrain. The body is a delta: flows divide and recombine; identity exists only through continual exchange.

Hamstring bruised and aching. A friend’s knee 3D-printed, replacing the one worn by skiing and fell running. We are all fighting the onslaught of entropy — gathering and metabolising energy in food, wine, humour, friendship, sunshine, music, sport, water, air and light.

Loosen the grip. Enter the flow.

---

## 02:00 — The pool at night

A swim amidst forks of lightning. Smoke clouds advance from the west. Submersion in dark water — the healing power that does not advertise itself.

Lightning emerging from connections. Reflecting. Refracting. Alive!

The pool is the central unit of this ambience: controlled nature; public yet private; a mirror that distorts; a place where voices sound different. Neither wilderness nor domestic interior — the holiday’s true unitary space.

---

## 03:00 — Smoke drifts east

Energy builds in the heat elsewhere. Sparks to the west lighting great fires where land meets sea. Warm breezes fan flames and drive smoke over fields and towns toward the hilled town with its basilica and bells.

We seek protection inside warm stone walls. We await further news. The spectacle of distant catastrophe arrives as atmosphere before it arrives as information.

---

## 04:00 — Temporary dwelling

Woken with the tune: *I Don’t Live Here Anymore*. We danced to Desolation Row — a reference to a reference. Not-belonging as the precise psychogeographic condition of the rented house.

The chalk door still holds the evening’s residue:

> Day ends  
> Darkness descends  
> The blue hour holds the warmth  
> Night awaits the blue hour

Silhouettes shape. Night awakes. The blue hour holds with memories old.

---

## 05:00 — Before emergence

Silence before people rising. Peaches in the bowl not yet warming. The rising sun / lowering sun line is still only a rumour at the shutters.

Vegetation floats in the swimming pool — living debris as process. The house as open system, metabolising externalities. Constant fight against entropy. Nothing is static. Everything is energy reorganising itself.

---

## 06:00 — Emergence

Rising blinds. Rising coffee. Rising temperature. People rising — awakening.

Silence emerging from noise. Wind from stillness. Light from shadows. Sleep from tiredness. Food from the land. Words from thoughts. Images from entangled chalk drawings. Meals from leftovers. Fuzzy consciousness from an evening of wine.

Awaking. Sitting with the morning. The house stretches before the heat.

---

## 07:00 — Transitions of the body

Sleep → wake → shower → coffee → water.

Sunlight reflected off the morning windows. The pool cover retreating — a mechanical dérive of its own. Otis and Griffo getting up. Submerging in still water.

Swim without headphones. Become porous. Do not write songs yet — collect thresholds: doorways, pool edges, garden gates, markets before opening, the silence after everyone has gone to bed.

---

## 08:00 — Market and olive

Surprise: the ability to buy food in the market. Olive flavour. Cleanliness of the pool. Hammock. Breeze mid-morning.

Food circulating. Coffee beginning its small social metabolism. Insects waking. People dispersing through rooms that are not theirs.

Stranger + language. What becomes possible because these two things meet?

---

## 09:00 — Encounters around the blue surface

Basilica bells ringing from the hilled town centre across the valley toward travellers from home meeting as friends around the pool — roast chicken, wine, beers, play, splashes, sun, music from a site radio, leaves rustling in the warm breeze.

We float above the cool blue surface of our lives in which light reflects and refracts — travelling through us, oblivious of the passage of time in its infinite arc across the universe, as we meet, eat, drink and talk.

Skin + water. Heat + stone. Child + echo. Tree + wind. Caffeinated + hydrated. Silence + sun. Railings + towels. Shutters + palm tree. Lizard + roof. Swing + shade.

---

## 10:00 — Causeways of the sun

Title seed from a book: *causeways of the sun*. Light playing in the pool — the image that refuses to leave — and its contrast with stone.

Preparation for a radically new environment, with its own internal landscape and logic, where old categories of thought would merely be an encumbrance.

Pandora: killing mouth and witch’s box of desires and frustrations, unpredictably opening and shutting the lid. The shutters of the house perform the same comedy.

---

## 11:00 — Otherness in the shade

Otis watching kids’ TV in French — otherness as ambient weather. Nancy and the pool. Child and adult sketching in chalk. The chalkboard records the coming and going of ideas as the house catalyses words, images, ideas.

Legs + hill. Coffee + milk. Food + wine. The delta synapse — neurological geography of morning heat.

AI as philosophical catalyst: synthesis; synthetic philosophy. The third thing that appears when two things that do not belong together meet.

---

## 12:00 — Midday stretch

The home stretches in the midday sun. Food eaten and rest won. The hammock brings peace amidst the sparrow’s song. The home rests in the beating sun.

Walls watch the drying grass. Water warms in the glass. Bodies stretched between the day’s hours that pass — as the pool brings cool at last.

Where did energy gather? In the heat round the pool. Sunlight — the pool beckons. Distant fusion taken in by clear water; rays reflect and refract; beauty made from borrowed energy.

---

## 13:00 — Right lunch

Right lunch! Whirls and eddies collecting vegetation and debris — the constant cleaning process as the house’s metabolism made visible.

A slower day: sitting in the slow flow of conversations and children playing in the pool. Heat pools. Conversation slows. Water absorbs energy. Birds begin to disappear.

Shades: light can enter but not heat. The architecture of filtered spectacle.

---

## 14:00 — Afternoon asleep

Regeneration in the sun’s heat. An afternoon asleep — the body as open system reclaiming what the morning spent.

Pulling a hamstring — getting old. Age and injury as forms of entropy the holiday cannot abolish, only metabolise. Starting to relax: the relationship with the holiday itself changing — from performance of leisure to passage through ambience.

“I’m living in Tewkesbury, just a normal guy.” Overheard speech as psychogeographic graffiti.

---

## 15:00 — Thresholds of stone and street

Walking street to street through Angoulême — crossing multiple thresholds in history. Passage of time and space present in stone buildings and narrow streets.

Doorways holding warm air. Shutters opening and closing. The door to the filter and pool pump. Sliding chalkboard door. The edge of the pool. The archway of the vine to the BBQ. Protective containment as the holiday’s true urbanism.

---

## 16:00 — Climbing memories return

Days of climbing in the Cotswolds and Wye Valley — gritstone gods and slate-punks of the 80s. Absence + memory. Head, hands, hips and feet compelled to move with the beat; rhythms ring; in the warm air the city hums.

Sun and drawing and meeting: shine combine / line combine.

The pool and the ball. Emersion. Flowing across the skin. Ripples. The sun reflecting.

---

## 17:00 — Mountain tales / new knee

Meeting old friends. Sharing tales of the mountains — Skye and the Aonach Eagach ridge, Tryfan, the peaks of the English lakes. Paella and good wine and company.

A new friend from an old acquaintance — exchange of memorabilia from times shared together forty years ago. History embedding in present company the way it embeds in stone.

Smoke drifts east; fires burn at a distance; we eat chicken and drink wine inside the warm stone walls.

---

## 18:00 — Energy begins to dissipate

Where did energy dissipate? In the cool of the evening. Breeze fights the heat of the sun — the sun will win, but night will descend to bring coolness again.

Roast Périgord chicken and white Bordeaux. Roast vegetables beside chilled wine and this morning’s bread. The evening succumbing to good company and the gentle sounds of mystic.

Joseph the storyteller creating stories for Otis, reading stories to Nancy. Narrative as another metabolism.

---

## 19:00 — Blue hour practice

Spend twenty minutes outside. Do not write. Just notice transitions.

Themes live inside transitions, not conclusions. Day ends. Darkness descends. The blue hour holds the warmth, unfolds. Silhouettes shape. Night awakes. The blue hour holds the liminal space between day and night.

The pool as liminal studio. Neither land nor sea. Controlled nature. Mirrors that distort.

---

## 20:00 — Insects louder than people

The sound of insects becoming louder than people. Smell of chlorine mixed with lavender. Temperature equalising. Smells travelling further. Voices becoming quieter. Bats replacing swallows.

Pétanque — although we’ve not played it yet. The unplayed game as pure potential, the dérive’s unfinished architecture.

Four questions only:

1. What surprised me?  
2. What remained unresolved?  
3. What relationship changed today?  
4. Which image refuses to leave?

---

## 21:00 — Chalk river to the sea

Drawing on the chalkboard. Images emerging from entangled chalk. Sliding door: chalk marks flowing like a river to the sea.

Child and adult sketching. The house processing conversations among friends. Four centuries of sound merging with tonight’s laughter.

Leave with fragments, not finished songs. Observations. Voice notes. Images. Melodic ideas. Lyric seeds. The songs may already have begun.

---

## 22:00 — Causeway closing

Heat, sun absorption — residue still in stone. The walls hold the day’s borrowed fusion a little longer than the air.

Sun → vines → grapes → wine → people → stories → songs.  
Sun → pool → evaporation → clouds → rain → rivers.

Nothing exists alone. Everything is borrowing energy. The songs are not about France; they are records of what became possible because a body and this place briefly interacted.

---

## 23:00 — Night awaits the blue hour

Again the chalkboard’s refrain. Night awaits. The blue hour already gone, already returning tomorrow.

We are the heat exchanging places. This afternoon assembled itself from chlorine, dust, insects and waiting. Nobody arrived — the afternoon became us instead.

The dérive does not conclude. It loops through the twenty-four units of ambience until the rented house releases its temporary dwellers back into the spectacle of ordinary time — carrying, if they have been porous enough, the third things that appeared when skin met water, when absence met memory, when friends met after forty years, when lightning met the dark surface of the pool.

Loosen the grip. Enter the flow. Fight entropy. Float.
