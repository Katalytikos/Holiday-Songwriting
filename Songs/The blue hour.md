## From Holiday Notes — 2026-07-22

### Thresholds
- Night awaits the blue hour
- Day ends, darkness descends; the blue hour holds the warmth, unfolds
- Silhouettes shape; night awakes; the blue hour holds the day’s memories (of old)
- (Transition?)

### Energy
- Energy dissipates in the cool of the evening
- Night descends to bring coolness again
- Insects feeding off warm skin after the day’s heat

### House as living process
- The house falls silent
- Plates of roast vegetables appear alongside the chilled wine and this morning’s bread
- The evening succumbs to good company and the gentle sounds of mystic

### Songwriting in Liminality
- Spend twenty minutes each evening outside during blue hour
- Don’t write — just notice transitions
- Themes live inside transitions, not conclusions
- The pool as liminal studio: neither land nor sea; public yet private; controlled nature; mirrors that distort; voices sounding different
